Subject: [CfS] GUGOG @ IEEE VIS 2026 — Grand Unified Grammar of Graphics Workshop

Dear colleagues,

We are pleased to announce the Call for Submissions for the Grand Unified
Grammar of Graphics (GUGOG) workshop at IEEE VIS 2026.

GUGOG is a half-day hybrid workshop that brings together researchers and
practitioners working on grammar-based visualization to seek a unified view
of a diverse landscape. While systems such as ggplot2, Vega-Lite, and
Observable Plot share intellectual roots in Wilkinson's Grammar of Graphics,
they differ substantially in their abstractions, primitives, design goals, and
target users. The workshop provides structured opportunities for cross-system
comparison, meta-theoretical reflection, and community consensus building that
the main conference program rarely affords.

--- SCOPE ---

Topics include, but are not limited to:
  - Comparative analyses of existing grammar systems
  - Formal and algebraic foundations of visualization grammars
  - Domain-specific grammars and critical reflections
  - Learnability, adoption, and natural language interfaces
  - User perspectives and practitioner experiences
  - Novel grammar extensions and ecosystems

--- SUBMISSION TYPES ---

Short position papers (less than 4 pages)
  Conceptual or philosophical arguments about grammar-based visualization.

Discussion abstracts (up to 500 words)
  Provocations, opinions, and observations on grammar-based visualization
  research.

All submissions are non-archival. Accepted work will be hosted on the workshop
website, and authors are encouraged to reuse content in follow-up publications.
Submissions are not anonymous.
