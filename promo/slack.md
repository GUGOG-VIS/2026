**Call for Submissions: GUGOG Workshop @ IEEE VIS 2026 (Deadline Aug 1)**

Dear Colleagues,

We invite you to submit your work to the **Grand Unified Grammar of Graphics (GUGOG)** workshop, a half-day hybrid workshop held at IEEE VIS 2026.

We welcome contributions from researchers working on programming languages, domain-specific languages, type systems, formal methods, and related areas who are interested in the foundations and design of grammar-based visualization systems. Systems such as ggplot2, Vega-Lite, and Observable Plot share intellectual roots in Wilkinson's Grammar of Graphics, yet differ substantially in their type systems, compositional semantics, abstraction layers, and execution models. The workshop provides a structured forum for cross-system comparison, formal analysis, and community consensus building.

**Submission Guidelines:** We are seeking original, unpublished, or ongoing work on topics including, but not limited to:
- Formal and algebraic semantics of visualization grammars
- Type systems, compositionality, and correctness guarantees for visual encodings
- DSL design, embedding strategies, and compiler/interpreter approaches
- Comparative analyses of grammar-based systems and their abstractions
- Domain-specific grammars (e.g., for ML pipelines, scientific data, geospatial)
- Natural language interfaces and LLM-driven chart generation
- Learnability, adoption, and practitioner experience

**Submission Types:**
- **Short position papers** (< 4 pages) — conceptual or technical arguments; formal treatments, DSL critiques, and cross-paradigm comparisons welcome
- **Discussion abstracts** (≤ 500 words) — provocations, open problems, and observations

All submissions are non-archival and not anonymous. Accepted work will be hosted on the workshop website, and authors are encouraged to reuse content in follow-up publications.
