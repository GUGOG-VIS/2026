**Andrew McNutt** is currently an assistant professor of computer science at the University of Utah, and an incoming professor at the University of British Columbia. His group builds tools, develops theories, and conduct studies that turn domain expertise (such as for making charts) into software that is approachable, trustworthy, and respectful of user agency. The work spans HCI, visualization, and programming interfaces. He has won multiple paper awards at HCI and visualization venues, received the Johanna Beyer EuroVis Early Career Award, and was a Siebel Scholar.
