**Josh Pollock** is a recent PhD graduate from the VIS group at MIT, advised by Arvind Satyanarayan. His research applies programming language ideas to visualization grammars, with a particular focus on finding unifying abstractions across domains. Josh’s work starts with the hypothesis that there is a universal, extensible language for data-driven graphics analogous to the lambda calculus or relational algebra. His research systems include GoFish and Bluefish, which unify UI, chart, and diagram abstractions; Animated Vega-Lite, which unifies interaction and animation for charts; and Benthic, which unifies abstractions for chart and diagram accessibility.
